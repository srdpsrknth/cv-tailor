🔍 Who we’re looking for
We are looking for a
Senior Data Analyst
to own supply analytics, partnering closely with our Supply Partnerships managers to make every supplier conversation, integration decision and commercial negotiation data-driven.About the role
You will be the analytical backbone of the Supply Partnerships team. Vio works with dozens of supply partners (OTAs, wholesalers, chains), and each one differs in availability, price competitiveness, commission terms and reliability. Your job is to make that landscape measurable and actionable.
This is a stakeholder-facing, ownership-heavy role. Rather than answering one-off questions, you will build the
self-serve tools, monitoring and metrics
that help partnerships managers answer most questions themselves and step in with deeper analysis, business cases and experiments where it matters most.
☑️ What you’ll do
Own supply performance metrics
Define, own and monitor the KPIs of each supply partner: bookings, GBV, commission rates, availability, price competitiveness, cancellation behaviour
Be the go-to person for what changed in supply performance and why
Detect and diagnose performance shifts proactively, before partners or leadership ask
Build tools for partnerships managers
Design and build self-serve dashboards, reports and analytical tools that partnerships managers use in day-to-day provider management and negotiations
Build
LLM-powered self-serve tools
- we believe LLM agents are the right interface for partnerships managers to answer their own data questions, and this role is expected to develop the skills to build them properly: encoding metric definitions, vetted queries and domain knowledge into agent skills so the answers are trustworthy
Standardise how provider performance is measured and communicated, internally and with partners
Reduce repetitive ad-hoc requests by productising the most common analyses
Business cases & commercial support
Build business cases and scenarios for commercial negotiations: commission tiers, rate access, new feed types, throttling and traffic allocation
Support prioritisation of new provider integrations with sizing and opportunity analysis
Help leadership shape the long-term supply strategy with data on inventory strengths, weaknesses and trends
Experimentation
Design and run A/B tests for supply-side changes (e.g. enabling a new feed, changing provider configuration) and communicate results and recommendations clearly
Know when a simple test is enough and when a question needs a different measurement approach
Raise the analytics bar
Share knowledge with other analysts and contribute to best practices across the analytics chapter
The team
You will work closely with:
Supply Partnerships managers, who own the relationships with our providers
Product managers and engineers working on provider integrations and the marketplace
A horizontal analytics chapter for knowledge sharing and peer review
You will report to
Gleb
- our Marketplace Analytics Lead.
